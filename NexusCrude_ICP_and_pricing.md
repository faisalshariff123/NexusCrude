# NexusCrude ICP and Pricing

## Ideal Customer Profile

### Core ICP

The best first customers are small to mid-sized research-heavy organizations that need Iraq or Middle East oil intelligence but cannot justify enterprise data subscriptions.

### Best-fit buyer segments

- Think tanks focused on MENA policy, energy, or security
- Energy consulting boutiques
- Geopolitical risk firms
- Academic research centers
- Independent analysts with paying clients

## Buyer Pain

These buyers usually face the same problems:

- Too many PDFs and scattered sources
- No clean structured Iraq oil dataset
- Expensive incumbent tools
- Need for fast source-grounded answers
- Manual report preparation and cross-checking

## Buying Trigger

Strong buying triggers include:

- Need to monitor Iraq output, exports, or disruptions continuously
- Need to produce internal briefs or client deliverables quickly
- Inability to afford incumbent platforms
- Desire to reduce analyst hours spent extracting data from reports

## Why They Buy

They buy when the tool saves analyst time, improves confidence in data, and makes recurring monitoring easier. The product must show practical time savings in the first week.

## Pricing Logic

Pricing should stay far below enterprise market-intelligence platforms while still being high enough to support a serious B2B product.

### Suggested pricing bands

| Plan | Monthly Price | Intended User |
|------|---------------|---------------|
| Starter | $149/month | Solo analysts, students, very small teams |
| Professional | $349/month | Think tanks, boutique consultancies, research teams |
| Team | $799/month | Multi-user organizations needing shared workflows |
| Custom | Custom quote | Organizations wanting tailored ingestion, seats, or reporting |

## Packaging Suggestions

### Starter

- Limited document uploads
- Limited AI queries per month
- Basic database access
- Standard exports

### Professional

- Higher query allowance
- Shared workspaces
- More source coverage
- Basic alerts and saved searches

### Team

- Multiple seats
- Shared folders and notes
- Analyst collaboration
- Priority support
- More frequent data refresh

### Custom

- Dedicated source ingestion
- Bespoke alerts
- Internal reporting workflows
- Optional training/onboarding

## Pricing Principles

- Do not underprice to the point where the product looks unserious.
- Early users are buying time savings, not raw rows in a table.
- Annual discounts can help with institutional buying cycles.
- If the product proves valuable, custom workflow and advisory-style features can raise ARPU.

## Early Sales Motion

The cleanest early motion is direct founder-led outreach to:

- Iraq/MENA energy researchers
- Small think tanks
- Boutique consultancies
- University-affiliated policy programs

Initial demos should focus on one thing: how much faster a buyer can answer a recurring Iraq oil question using NexusCrude compared with manual PDF review.
