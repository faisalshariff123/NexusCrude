# NexusCrude Project Brief

## Overview

NexusCrude is a B2B SaaS platform focused on Iraq and broader Middle East oil intelligence. The product is designed for think tanks, energy consultants, academic researchers, regional policy analysts, and smaller research firms that need structured oil-market data and analysis without paying enterprise pricing for platforms like OPEC data terminals, Argus, or Wood Mackenzie.

The core product thesis is that public-source oil data is abundant but fragmented. EIA, OPEC, EITI, IEA, Iraqi Ministry of Oil releases, company reports, and reputable regional news outlets all publish useful information, but the data is scattered across PDFs, websites, and periodic bulletins. NexusCrude aggregates these sources, extracts structured facts, normalizes them, and exposes them through dashboards, search, AI-assisted Q&A, and analyst workflows.

## Problem

Current options are either too expensive, too fragmented, or too manual:

- Enterprise oil intelligence platforms are priced for large corporates, trading desks, and major consultancies.
- Public data exists, but researchers waste time manually reading PDFs and stitching together numbers.
- Iraq-specific oil information is often mixed across export reports, production summaries, geopolitical updates, and transparency disclosures.
- Smaller organizations want answers fast, but they do not have internal data engineering teams.

## Target Users

### Primary users

- Middle East policy think tanks
- Iraq-focused energy researchers
- Independent consultants and boutique advisory firms
- University research groups
- Risk and geopolitical analysis teams

### Secondary users

- Corporate strategy teams tracking Iraq/MENA energy developments
- Journalists covering regional oil issues
- Investors and analysts following oil infrastructure and export trends

## Core Value Proposition

NexusCrude gives smaller teams an affordable way to:

- Track Iraq oil production, exports, infrastructure, revenue, and disruptions
- Query structured data from public documents and reports
- Ask natural-language questions and receive source-grounded answers
- Compare conflicting figures across institutions like EIA, OPEC, and EITI
- Build internal reports faster with fewer manual steps

## Initial Product Wedge

The starting wedge is Iraq oil intelligence. Iraq is a strong entry point because it has large strategic importance, significant public reporting, ongoing geopolitical relevance, and a research audience that needs current structured information. Once the product proves useful for Iraq, the platform can expand into GCC and wider MENA coverage.

## Product Surface

Likely first-version product areas:

- Data ingestion from PDFs and official/public web sources
- Structured extraction of production, export, revenue, and infrastructure data
- Searchable database with source references
- AI question answering grounded in internal data
- Analyst notes and exportable research summaries
- Basic charts, timelines, and event tracking

## Strategic Positioning

NexusCrude is not a trading terminal and should not try to compete head-on with full enterprise commodities platforms. It should position itself as an affordable, research-first oil intelligence product for smaller teams that need speed, structure, and explainability.

## Near-Term Priorities

1. Build reliable ingestion for Iraq-focused source documents.
2. Normalize extracted production/export metrics into a clean database.
3. Launch a source-grounded query interface.
4. Validate with a small set of domain users.
5. Tighten pricing, packaging, and reporting workflows based on usage.
